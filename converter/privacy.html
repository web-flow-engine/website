Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

Introduction

VideoConverts ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle data when you use our application.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. VideoConverts operates entirely on your device. All file conversions are performed locally, and no files or data are transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

Data Usage

Since we do not collect any data, we do not use, process, or analyze any user information. Your files remain on your device at all times.

Data Storage

All converted files are stored locally on your device in locations you choose. We do not store any copies of your files on external servers or cloud storage.

Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties. Because no data leaves your device, there is nothing to share, sell, or transmit to any external entity.

Third-Party Services

VideoConverts does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services. The app functions as a standalone tool with no external dependencies that could access your data.

Children's Privacy

VideoConverts does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any data from any users, this is inherently protected.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
