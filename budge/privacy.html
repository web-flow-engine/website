Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 26, 2026

Data Collection

Budge is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal data to provide our services:

  • Expense Data: All expense entries, categories, amounts, dates, and notes you enter are stored locally on your device.
  • Profile Information: Optional profile data you choose to enter, such as name and currency preferences.
  • App Settings: Your preferences for categories, budgets, and display settings.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personal identifying information unless you voluntarily provide it through our support contact.

Data Usage

We use the data we collect solely to provide and improve the Budge app:

  • Core Functionality: Your expense data is used to display reports, generate insights, and help you track your spending.
  • App Improvement: We may analyze aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to improve app performance and user experience.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.
  • No Advertising: Budge does not display ads and does not share data with advertising networks.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on your device:

  • Local Storage: All expense data is stored locally on your iOS device using Apple's secure storage mechanisms.
  • iCloud Backup: If enabled, data may be backed up to your personal iCloud account. This backup is under your control and encrypted by Apple.
  • No Cloud Servers: We do not store your expense data on our servers. Your financial information remains on your device.
  • Data Retention: Your data remains on your device until you choose to delete it or uninstall the app.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete Data: You can delete any expense entry or category at any time within the app.
  • Export Data: You can export all your data as CSV or PDF files for your records.
  • Stop Data Collection: Simply stop using the app or delete it to stop all data collection.
  • Access Your Data: All your data is accessible and viewable within the app at any time.
  • Request Support: Contact us at support@budge.app for any data-related questions or requests.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or our data practices, please contact us at:
